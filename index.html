Why Handloom

Woven slower, worn longer.

  • Pure fibre, no shortcuts

    Cotton and wool only — no synthetic blends that pill or trap heat.

  • Made to a rhythm, not a deadline

    Each piece is set on the loom by hand, thread by thread — small batches, real texture.

  • Colours that hold

    Colourfast dyeing means the wash after wash story stays the same as day one.
